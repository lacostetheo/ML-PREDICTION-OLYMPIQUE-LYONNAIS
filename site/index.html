What is actually running

The model, in plain words

CHOSEN MODEL

Multinomial logistic regression

One linear formula per outcome (home win, draw, away win) on 100 inputs, turned into probabilities by a softmax. Re-fitted every season on all past matches, with the strength of its regularisation chosen by time-ordered cross-validation.

  • As accurate as the alternatives. Gradient boosting, random forest and neural networks were tested: none beats it significantly (averaging all three gains a negligible 0.0014 log loss).
  • Explainable. Every forecast splits exactly into the contribution of Elo, squad value, form, shots… — that is what the Match Lab shows.
  • Honest. Its probabilities are well calibrated: a "70 %" forecast comes true about 70 % of the time.

From data to forecast

What about neural networks?

Tested, not retained. A 100 → 64 → 32 → 3 network scored 0.9796 vs 0.9793 for the logistic regression (statistically tied) and needed far more tuning. Illustration of the architecture only — not the learned weights.

Glossary

Elo rating
A strength score updated after every match: beat a strong team and it jumps, lose to a weak one and it drops.
Log loss
Penalises confident wrong forecasts heavily. Lower is better; 1.075 = ignoring team quality, 0.966 = the bookmaker.
Calibration
Do forecasts mean what they say? Among matches given 70 %, ~70 % should happen.
Walk-forward
Train on the past, predict the next season, repeat. Never test on data older than the training set.
Leakage
Using information from after the match. An automated test checks that the pipeline cannot.
Monte Carlo
Play the rest of the season thousands of times with the model's probabilities and count what happens.

Limits you should know

  • No injuries, line-ups or coaching news — the likely reason the bookmaker is better.
  • Draws are essentially unpredictable: the model almost never names a draw as the most likely outcome.
  • Team strength is a snapshot of —. Results of 23–25 October 2026 were not yet in the data source.
  • Calendars beyond the next round come from a public Ligue 1 calendar (dates can change); other leagues: choose the opponent.
  • Academic project — not betting advice.
